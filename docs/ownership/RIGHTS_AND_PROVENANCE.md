## Recorded chronology

Historical Git commits and previous GitHub Action runs retain their original
dates. This policy is first committed in the project on **2026-10-16**, not
backdated. A GitHub Action separately records the *actual execution clock*
in UTC and America/New_York and calculates SHA-256 digests of every tracked
regular file at that exact commit revision. Evidence is uploaded as a CI
artifact and cryptographically attested with GitHub's signed build-provenance
mechanism. A future attestation log records the time it was made; it is not
an independent proof of the historical creation time or personal authorship.

The proof record will contain: account/repository identity, triggering actor,
commit SHA, GitHub run URL, actual recorded UTC and Eastern timestamps, SHA-256
file hashes, aggregate manifest SHA-256, rights-claim limitations, and third-party
review status. It names Timothy Harris publicly as the self-reported claimant, but does not contain signatures, addresses, government IDs, passwords, or other private records.

## Human contribution and AI assistance

Keep provenance and substantive human selection, design, modeling,
revision/arrangement, and creative decisions for code, visuals and game
assets where possible. Do not declare all automatically generated source,
images, meshes or models copyrightable. Human selection alone is not
always sufficient. The U.S. Copyright Office has specific human-authorship
rules for works involving generative AI.

## Legal finalization checklist

1. Review the [signable project declaration](OWNER_DECLARATION_TO_SIGN.md)
   **privately**. Add the actual claimant/author legal name and signing date;
   collect signatures or written assignments from anyone who contributed rights
   the claimant does not already own. Do not commit sensitive signatures or
   personal identifying details to this public repository.
2. Verify all third-party dependencies, downloaded language models, textures,
   creative assets, tools and licenses using THIRD_PARTY_REGISTER.md.
3. Decide whether VQ should move to a private repository; public
   publication cannot undo past access or the GitHub Terms' viewing/forking
   provisions. Keep offline encrypted backups of Git history, manifests and
   signed records.
4. Have qualified U.S. intellectual-property counsel review authorship,
   ownership, prior licenses, contractor assignments, trademark strategy and
   whether patent filing is appropriate before public disclosure of genuinely
   novel inventions.
5. For original copyrightable software/visual work, consider U.S. Copyright
   Office registration at https://copyright.gov/registration/. Registration
   is separate from timestamps, Git commits and an internal declaration.
6. Once signed and third-party review is resolved, store the legally executed
   agreement in a secure private vault, referencing GitHub commit IDs and
   signed provenance artifacts. Avoid public disclosure of private signatures.

## Evidence limits

No commit message, copyright notice or CI attestation makes an unsigned
copyright transfer valid, and no documentation converts unprotectable ideas
or uncopyrightable AI-only expression into exclusive copyright. Records help
prove *what was in a repository, when the system observed it, and which
account controlled that repository*. They are useful evidence, not a judicial
determination or a substitute for a lawyer.
